# Research-only BoneHub metacarpal derivatives

Source: Hamid Alavi and Malte Asseln (University of Twente), Visible Human Full-Skeleton 3D Bone Models, DOI https://doi.org/10.57967/hf/10464.

Pinned source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/BoneHub/visible-human-3d-models/tree/7d520ffe410b3f79d82991b8368b2d51844feb80

License: Creative Commons Attribution4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The pinned dataset README, file hashes and retrieval records are in the preceding `geometry-recovery-bone-methods-2026-10-22T07-10-36-377Z-kDATcr` evidence directory.

Underlying image provenance: NLM Visible Human male; aligned CT redistributed by Andreassen et al. (2023), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01905-2 . BoneHub is an independent segmentation; it is not a BodyParts3D model.

Changes by Myology3D: one proper rigid transformation per bone, no scale/reflection/deformation; STL-to-GLB conversion; explicit vertex normals in v2. Exact transforms and hashes are retained. BP3D reference geometry is unchanged, used only for research comparison; existing BP3D derivative license obligations remain separate.

These models remain TEST ONLY, not anatomically admitted or connected to the main atlas. Known intersections with existing neighbours persist. Acknowledgement of source does not imply endorsement by the authors.
